My Journey

I didn't start with a perfect system.

I started January 1st, 2025. I was trying to figure out trading from the ground up, and like most beginners, I thought the answer was somewhere inside the next video, the next strategy or the next indicator.

I spent months learning pieces of the market without understanding how those pieces belonged together. I could recognize terminology, but recognition wasn't the same thing as execution.

Eventually, mentorship and experience taught me something I wish I had understood earlier: trading isn't just about finding an entry. It's about having a repeatable process, managing risk, accepting when you're wrong and controlling the decisions you make when money is on the line.

That changed the way I studied the charts. I stopped trying to predict every candle and started building a framework around context, location, confirmation and execution.

Today, my main focus is Gold (XAUUSD), particularly the New York session. I'm still learning and refining the craft, but I no longer believe beginners need more noise. They need a clear path through the noise.

“I don't want to teach you how to chase trades. I want to teach you how to think through one.”

The Rules

Simple rules protect complicated emotions.

✓Wait for your setup

No setup means no trade. Being in the market is not the goal.

✓Define risk first

Know your invalidation and acceptable loss before entering.

✓Don't chase

Missing a move is better than creating a setup that isn't there.

✓Review honestly

Separate a bad outcome from a bad decision. Review the process.
